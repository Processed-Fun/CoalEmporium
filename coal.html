Our coal

Not all coal carries the same message. We stock four grades, sorted by hand at our Scranton facility, so the quality of the coal can match the quality of the behavior.

The four grades

Grade selection is a matter of judgment. As a rule: the worse the conduct, the better the coal. A recipient who has truly earned their gift deserves to know it at a glance.

A note on sourcing

All Coal Emporium product is purchased as surplus and reclaimed stock from legacy Pennsylvania operations. Nothing we sell is mined for us. Every lump we ship is coal that already existed and simply needed a purpose. We believe giving it to a child who bit their dentist is a dignified second life for the material.

Gift coal is sold for sentimental and instructional use. It arrives washed, dried, and sealed, and it will not mark clothing under normal unwrapping conditions. It is not packaged, graded, or priced as heating fuel, and at our prices you would not want it to be.

Quality control

Each lump passes through a three station inspection line where staff check weight, structural integrity, and general disposition. Lumps that appear too cheerful are returned to the hopper. Our reject rate in 2025 was 11 percent, which we consider the cost of doing this correctly.
