Frequently asked questions

Compiled from 38 years of phone calls. If your question is not answered here, our consultants are available at 1-800-555-0142.

Is this a joke?

No. We are a coal gifting company. We sell coal, for gifts.

My child cried. Is that normal?

Yes. Crying indicates the message was received. In our January surveys, households reporting tears on the 25th also report the largest behavioral improvements by March. Comfort the child, then point to the voucher if you purchased one.

How do I know how much coal my child deserves?

Use the recommender on our packages page, or call the Naughtiness Assessment Hotline and describe the year's incidents to a consultant. Be specific. "He's been a handful" gets you a follow up question. "He sold his sister's bike to a classmate" gets you a weight.

Can I mix coal with regular gifts?

We advise against it. Mixed messaging is the leading cause of repeat orders. A stocking that holds both coal and a gift card tells the child that consequences are negotiable. If the year had bright spots, our January Redemption Voucher preserves the incentive structure without diluting the morning itself.

What if my child was actually good this year?

Then you do not need us, and we are glad for you. Some customers order a Warning Shot to keep on the shelf, unwrapped and visible, as a standing reference. We call this a deterrent placement and it is fully within the intended use of the product.

Do you ship to adults?

Roughly a quarter of our volume goes to recipients over eighteen. Spouses, in-laws, adult children, and group chat administrators are all served by our adult line. Freight deliveries require a signature, which our customers consistently describe as the best part.

Will the recipient know it came from me?

Only if you want them to. The gift tag can carry your name, remain blank, or read "You know why." The third option is chosen by 61 percent of adult line customers.

What is your return policy?

We do not accept returns of coal once behavior has improved, as the product has performed as intended. Unopened packages may be exchanged within 30 days for a package of equal or greater naughtiness. Refunds are issued only when our Assessment team concludes the recipient was, on review, nice.

Is the coal safe to handle?

Yes. Every lump is washed, dried, and sealed before packing, and children's packages contain no lump small enough to swallow or large enough to throw effectively. Coal is a rock. Treat it like one. Wash hands after extended handling, and do not permit the recipient to eat it out of spite. It has happened.

Can we burn it afterward?

Gift coal is sold for sentimental and instructional purposes and is not graded as fuel. Most families keep the lump. Some engrave the year on it. One customer in Vermont has a mantel arrangement covering 2011 through 2024, with a gap in 2017 that the family does not discuss.

When is the order deadline for Christmas?

December 18 for standard packages, December 15 for freight. Orders placed after the deadline ship with a card reading "Your coal is on its way," which several customers have told us was somehow worse. We print the cards anyway.

Do you offer gift receipts?

Yes. The gift receipt lists the package, the assessed naughtiness level, and the phrase "exchanges permitted, explanations required." Recipients who attempt an exchange are connected with our Assessment team, and most withdraw the request within the first three questions.

My spouse and I disagree about the appropriate tonnage. Can you help?

This is the most common December call. Our consultants will hear both accounts and issue a written recommendation. The recommendation is binding on no one, but it is on letterhead, and in most households that settles it.

Is there a subscription option?

The Perennial Plan ships a Standard Stocking every December until canceled. Families enroll after the second consecutive coal year. Cancellation requires two consecutive years of documented good behavior or one heartfelt letter from the recipient, reviewed case by case.

Still have questions?

Describe the behavior. We will take it from there.
