About Coal Emporium

Thirty eight years of telling children the truth, by weight.

Our history

Coal Emporium was founded in Scranton, Pennsylvania in 1987 by Harold Pfeiffer, a third generation heating coal distributor. That December, a neighbor asked Harold for a single lump to put in her son's stocking. The boy had shaved the cat. Harold selected the lump personally, wrapped it in newspaper, and refused payment.

By January he had received eleven more requests, two of them from out of state. Harold recognized what the heating market had stopped being and what the gifting market had not yet become. He printed a one page catalog, bought a scale, and never sold another furnace load.

Today the company remains family owned. We operate a 40,000 square foot sorting and fulfillment facility on Culm Bank Road, employ 63 people year round and roughly 200 each December, and ship to all fifty states. The original lump, recovered from the neighbor's estate sale in 2009, is displayed in our lobby.

Our mission

We believe consequences make better gifts than most gifts do. A toy is forgotten by February. A lump of coal, delivered with love and proper documentation, is remembered at every family gathering for the rest of the recipient's life. We exist to make that moment available to every household at a fair price.

What we stand for

  • Honest weights. Every package is weighed twice. If we say two pounds, the child receives two pounds.
  • Proportional response. We will decline an order when the described behavior does not support the requested tonnage. It happens more often than you would think.
  • Discretion. Outer packaging is unmarked. Until the morning of the 25th, nobody knows.
  • Second chances. Our January Redemption Voucher program has converted coal into improved behavior in four out of ten documented cases.
