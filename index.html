How gifting coal works

The process is simple. Most customers complete an order in under ten minutes, including the behavioral review.

1

Assess the behavior

Review the recipient's conduct over the calendar year. Our naughtiness scale runs from 1 (talked back once) to 10 (you know what they did). Be honest. Underestimating helps no one.

2

Select package and grade

Match the naughtiness level to a package weight, then choose a coal grade. Economy Lignite covers borderline cases. Premium Anthracite is for conduct that warrants the best.

3

We handle delivery

Your coal arrives clean, gift ready, and discreetly boxed before December 24. Stocking sized lumps are individually wrapped in tissue paper at no extra charge.
